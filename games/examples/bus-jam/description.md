# My Description

There's a group of colored buses and a crowd of people at a bus stop. As new colored busses arrive you have to tap to match the people to the bus that matches their color. So, red people in the red bus, pink in the pink, etc. You can't pick just any bus. You have to get the specific ones that people need to clear the next area. You have to be strategic to not block paths.
