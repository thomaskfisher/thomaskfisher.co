# My Description

This is a survival game where you start with one fighter. That shoots at an oncoming wave of bad guys. You start out with just a single guy and shooting slowly. The shooting just happens automatically; you can drag your finger left and right to control where he is on the rolling map. There are barriers and power-ups to grow your army, which increases the rate of fire or does other things. It's really just a strategy game based around being able to quickly do math and figure out where you should be shooting in order to bring down a target. The targets have a number associated with them. And each time you hit it with a shot, that number decreases, and you only get the power up when that number goes to zero. Assuming you can do that before it rolls past you. If a bad guy touches you/your army the level ends.
