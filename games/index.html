Games

No ads. No accounts. No tracking. Nothing leaves your phone, and they all work offline.

  • Color Sort Pour liquids until every tube holds one color. Endless, and never unsolvable.
  • Screw Land Unscrew the plates in the right order. Every level comes apart — guaranteed.
  • Bus Jam Walk the crowd onto the right buses. Every level has a way through.
  • Survival Route your squad through the gates and outnumber the horde. Take your time.
New to one of these? Every game explains itself the first time you open it, and the ? in its top corner has the rules again whenever you want them.

Add one to your home screen (Share → Add to Home Screen) and it opens like an app.
